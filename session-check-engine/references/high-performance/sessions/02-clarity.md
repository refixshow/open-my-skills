# S02 Clarity

## Session purpose

Develop intentional behavioral clarity around who the client wants to be, how they want to interact, how they recognize success, and what matters enough to guide weekly action.

## In-scope threads

- present certainty and uncertainty;
- current values and alignment between identity and life;
- the client's own explanation of past success;
- three words for self, social interactions, and success markers, with reasons;
- identity, relationships, skills, service, desired emotions, and meaningful contribution;
- reminders or weekly practices that turn chosen qualities into behavior.

## Core checkpoints

1. Start from the client's current experience of clarity and ambiguity.
2. Elicit what the client values rather than supplying desirable values.
3. Translate identity language into observable interaction and success markers.
4. Explore why the chosen qualities matter.
5. Convert clarity into a reminder, practice, or near-term behavior.

## Coach agency

Use either the identity declaration, the visual clarity card, or a conversational equivalent. Depth and ownership matter more than completing every box.

## Out-of-scope drift

Do not replace clarity work with a lecture on influence, persuasion, productivity, or courage. A brief question about whom the client wants to serve is in scope when it clarifies identity or meaning.

## Review signals

- strong: abstract values become client-owned behavioral markers;
- partial: appealing words are selected without reasons or translation into behavior;
- drift: the coach defines the client's identity, teaches another pillar, or rushes to an action plan before clarity exists.
