# S07 Mindset

## Session purpose

Help the client notice chosen perspectives and reactions, reconnect with strengths, and design an intentional mental habit without denying reality.

## In-scope threads

- how the client's thinking has supported past success;
- an empowering belief or perspective grounded in experience;
- periods of excessive negative focus and their present influence;
- a healthier perspective and a repeatable reminder or practice;
- five strengths, overemphasized limitations, and more deliberate use of strengths;
- optional observation notes across purpose, presence, physiology, performance, and persuasion.

## Core checkpoints

1. Start with the client's evidence about how they currently think and react.
2. Identify both useful perspectives and costly focus patterns.
3. Ground strengths and beliefs in concrete examples.
4. Choose a realistic reminder, reorientation, or practice.
5. Preserve responsibility without claiming control over every event or emotion.

## Coach agency

Use a strength-based or perspective-based route according to the client. Challenge absolutist conclusions while validating real loss, constraint, and emotion.

## Out-of-scope drift

Do not re-run the clarity, courage, influence, or persuasion module. Do not turn mindset into forced positivity, blame, affirmations without evidence, or a lecture about successful celebrities.

## Review signals

- strong: the client generates an evidence-based perspective and practice;
- partial: a positive statement appears without connection to the client's experience;
- drift: the coach invalidates emotion, overstates personal control, or explains every obstacle as mindset.

## Safety and limits

Mindset language is not a diagnosis or treatment. Do not use it to dismiss grief, discrimination, trauma, illness, or material constraints.
