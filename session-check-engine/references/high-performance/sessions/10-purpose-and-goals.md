# S10 Purpose and goals

## Session purpose

Help the client define an evolving life-purpose declaration that joins ways of being, meaningful action, contribution, and the reason those choices matter.

## In-scope threads

- how the client's purpose has changed through the program;
- periods when life felt misaligned and what was learned;
- emotions associated with meaningful daily action;
- whether important people understand the client's purpose;
- helping others without imposing the client's answer;
- purpose as defined and lived, not passively found;
- elements of being, doing, contribution, and `so that` meaning;
- a concise declaration and a way to embody or share it.

## Core checkpoints

1. Review how purpose and alignment have evolved.
2. Normalize multiple or changing purposes across life stages and domains.
3. Elicit desired ways of being and doing.
4. Clarify contribution and why it matters.
5. Form a client-owned declaration and connect it to lived behavior.

## Coach agency

Allow broad language when it genuinely orients the client. Do not force one permanent slogan, a career-only mission, or artificial specificity.

## Out-of-scope drift

Do not re-run all of S02 Clarity or turn this into operational quarterly goal planning. Concrete action may embody the declaration, but project planning belongs elsewhere unless explicitly contracted.

## Review signals

- strong: the declaration integrates being, doing, contribution, and personal meaning;
- partial: the statement sounds polished but has no lived significance or client ownership;
- drift: the coach supplies the purpose, insists it must be singular and permanent, or converts the session into SMART goals.

## Safety and limits

Do not treat uncertainty about life purpose as pathology or failure. Avoid spiritual, moral, or career prescriptions not chosen by the client.
