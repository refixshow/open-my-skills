# S00 Strategy session

## Session purpose

Establish an honest starting point for the program: current work and life context, priority goals, stressors, desired changes, motivation, and the client's picture of a better life.

## In-scope threads

- current activities and the path that led to them;
- three important goals and current challenges;
- sources of happiness, pride, excitement, and fulfillment;
- desired life, obstacles, abandoned aspirations, and three changes for the next year;
- the client's own reason for entering High Performance work.

## Core checkpoints

1. Contract the session as a baseline, not a judgment.
2. Understand the client's current world before selecting improvement areas.
3. Elicit desired direction and present constraints in the client's language.
4. Surface motivation and commitment without manufacturing urgency.
5. Close with a usable starting picture for later sessions.

## Coach agency

Adapt the intake order to the client's narrative. Summarize patterns and tensions instead of mechanically asking all ten source questions. Preserve enough breadth to establish a baseline.

## Out-of-scope drift

A short overview of the program pillars is in scope. Detailed teaching or exercises from clarity, energy, courage, productivity, or influence are premature when they replace baseline discovery.

## Review signals

- strong: the client, not the coach, defines the current map and reasons for change;
- partial: goals are collected but meaning, stressors, or desired life remain unclear;
- drift: the session becomes a broad motivational lecture or starts solving one later pillar in depth.

Do not treat the baseline as a diagnostic assessment. Escalate material outside ordinary coaching scope rather than converting it into a performance goal.
