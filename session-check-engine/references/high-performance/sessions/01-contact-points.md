# S01 Contact points

## Session purpose

Create a structured map of present focus, life areas, perceived potential or control, and habits that support or obstruct progress.

## In-scope threads

- life areas needing focus;
- three-month personal and professional goals and their importance;
- distractions and competing areas of attention;
- areas that feel solid, need improvement, or feel outside control;
- current supportive habits, desired habits, and habits needed for a much stronger year;
- optional life-area self-rating used for reflection, not labeling.

## Core checkpoints

1. Establish what deserves focus now.
2. Separate personal and professional goals while testing why they matter.
3. Map solid areas, improvement areas, and perceived lack of control.
4. Identify existing and missing habits.
5. Select a small number of implications rather than trying to repair every low area.

## Coach agency

Use either conversational mapping or the worksheet. Follow the most consequential discrepancy and return to the broader map before closing.

## Out-of-scope drift

Naming the five program pillars is allowed for orientation. Teaching a full clarity, energy, courage, productivity, or influence module is outside this session's mapping function.

## Review signals

- strong: the client leaves with a prioritized map and observable habit focus;
- partial: scores are gathered but not interpreted with the client;
- drift: the coach prescribes work based on the lowest score or treats the score as an objective measure.

Treat all ratings as subjective snapshots. Do not infer mental health, relationship quality, financial health, or medical status from a scale score.
