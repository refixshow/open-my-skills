# S09 Persuasion

## Session purpose

Prepare an ethical, specific persuasive interaction by recognizing the other person's reality, connecting to meaningful ambition, and communicating with authentic emotional engagement.

## In-scope threads

- situations where the client tries to persuade and what has or has not worked;
- strengths and gaps in persuasive communication;
- the concrete person, belief, action, request, and reason for influence;
- recognition of the other person's reality;
- internal and external aspirations or rewards;
- affect through tone, story, and emotional connection;
- mutual benefit, consequences, options, and a realistic time frame.

## Core checkpoints

1. Choose one real interaction and define the desired belief or commitment.
2. Understand the other person's reality before constructing the request.
3. Connect the proposal to aspirations meaningful to that person.
4. Plan emotionally congruent communication without manufacturing emotion.
5. Test mutual benefit, autonomy, foreseeable consequences, and realistic timing.

## Coach agency

Use the recognition-ambition-affect formula as a planning lens, not a script. Invite the client to anticipate objections and alternatives without rehearsing pressure tactics.

## Out-of-scope drift

Do not retreat into a general lecture on influence or repeat the full S08 model. Do not broaden to every relationship when one concrete persuasive situation can be tested.

## Review signals

- strong: the client understands the other party, clarifies mutual value, and prepares a specific ethical request;
- partial: the message is polished but the other person's perspective or freedom to decline is absent;
- drift: the plan relies on fear, social pressure, misleading consequences, or emotional manipulation.

## Safety and limits

Respect informed choice, power differences, confidentiality, and the right to refuse. Do not assist coercive, deceptive, discriminatory, exploitative, or unsafe persuasion.
