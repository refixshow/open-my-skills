# S03 Energy

## Session purpose

Help the client observe physical, mental, and spiritual energy patterns, identify supports and drains, and choose a small set of appropriate practices.

## In-scope threads

- energy over recent months and a remembered high-energy period;
- sleep, movement, nutrition, well-being, and other client-identified supports;
- daily, weekly, monthly, and annual recharge patterns;
- stressors and energy drains;
- what greater usable energy would enable;
- one or more practices to begin, stop, or test.

## Core checkpoints

1. Establish the client's lived energy pattern and context.
2. Explore multiple energy dimensions without presenting them as measurements.
3. Link energy to meaningful activity rather than energy for its own sake.
4. Identify supports, drains, and recovery cycles.
5. Select no more than a few feasible experiments with client ownership.

## Coach agency

Use the checklist as a menu, not a prescription. Tailor language to the client's health, constraints, preferences, and professional guidance.

## Out-of-scope drift

Detailed work on body language and physical presence belongs to S04. Productivity planning belongs to S06. Do not use either as a substitute for understanding the energy pattern.

## Review signals

- strong: observation leads to a client-chosen experiment and review point;
- partial: the session names many healthy behaviors but does not prioritize or tailor them;
- drift: the coach gives generic health instructions, promises an effect, or bypasses medical constraints.
