# S04 Physiological posture

## Session purpose

Connect energy and self-presentation with observable physical presence: posture, voice, gesture, facial expression, recovery, and activation.

## In-scope threads

- energy changes since the earlier energy session;
- the client's experience of posture, voice, gesture, facial expression, and presentation;
- alignment between intended identity and visible or audible presence;
- an optional relaxation or activation exercise;
- a sustainable personal practice and selected energy-supporting behaviors.

## Core checkpoints

1. Follow up on prior energy experiments before adding techniques.
2. Invite the client's observation of physical presence without mind-reading.
3. Choose one relevant dimension to practice.
4. Obtain consent and check comfort before breath, movement, voice, or eyes-closed exercises.
5. Debrief the experience and let the client choose what, if anything, to continue.

## Coach agency

Adapt or omit scripted exercises when context, access needs, setting, health, or client preference calls for it. A useful debrief matters more than performance intensity.

## Out-of-scope drift

Do not turn the session into appearance criticism, a persuasion lesson, an influence lecture, or a medical-energy protocol. Any cross-reference must serve the client's chosen presence goal.

## Review signals

- strong: the coach asks, obtains consent, observes without judgment, and integrates learning;
- partial: an exercise is completed but not connected to the client's aim;
- drift: the coach imposes aesthetics, interprets body language as hidden truth, or presses through discomfort.

## Safety and limits

Breathwork, movement, meditation, diet, supplements, sleep, and exercise may have contraindications. Do not pressure participation or present physical appearance as moral worth, trustworthiness, or proof of competence.
