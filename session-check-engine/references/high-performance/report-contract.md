# High Performance review contract

## Required opening

State:

- the reviewed transcript and declared or inferred session topic;
- speaker and timestamp reliability;
- whether the transcript appears complete;
- that findings are supervision hypotheses, not a competence verdict.

## Required sections

### Executive assessment

Give a short conclusion on whether the session primarily served its declared High Performance function. Name the strongest alignment and the most important risk. Do not reduce the assessment to a numeric score.

### What worked

Name 2-4 transcript-supported moments where the coach protected the topic, followed the client, made an effective teaching move, preserved ownership, or achieved a source function through adaptation.

### First-sieve findings

Return 2-5 findings ordered by impact. For each include:

1. **Session moment** - timestamp or line reference and a minimal locating quote.
2. **Observation** - what happened in the transcript.
3. **High Performance function** - expected function from the primary session card.
4. **Assessment** - `aligned`, `partially achieved`, `missed opportunity`, or `scope drift`.
5. **Why it matters** - effect on the current session, not on coaching in general.
6. **Better next move** - one adapted question, reflection, contracting move, or concise frame.
7. **Alternative reading** - why the current move may have been reasonable or why context is insufficient.
8. **Source basis** - session ID plus PDF page and/or instructor-audio timestamp.
9. **Confidence** - `high`, `medium`, or `low` with one-sentence rationale.

### Topic-discipline check

List any meaningful cross-topic bridges or drift. If none occurred, state that the session stayed within scope. Do not criticize a brief reference merely because another pillar was named.

### Practice priorities

Give 1-3 observable coach behaviors to rehearse. Tie each to the declared session.

## Language and tone

Write in the user's language. Prefer direct supervision language: `najważniejsza poprawka`, `funkcja sesji została osiągnięta`, `warto przetestować`. Avoid shaming language and avoid praising script recitation.
