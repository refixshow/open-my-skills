# Source, privacy, and methodology policy

## Source model

The corpus contains 13 PDFs and 116 MP4 files, of which 115 are unique, plus two user-provided post-session sheet images. A session may have one core PDF, optional worksheets, and several instructor recordings. Do not assume one audio per PDF.

Use:

- `HP-SNN-PDF` for a primary session document;
- named worksheet PDF IDs where present;
- `HP-ANNN-AUDIO` for one unique instructor recording.

Resolve IDs through [source-manifest.md](source-manifest.md). Cite PDF pages and recording timestamps. Preserve disagreements between sources. Consult [source-discrepancies.md](source-discrepancies.md) before treating a filename or isolated page label as the session identity.

## Distillation rule

Store only concise original procedural knowledge in the skill: purpose, checkpoints, acceptable adaptations, scope boundaries, review criteria, safety limits, and provenance. Keep full PDFs, MP4s, extracted pages, and lecture transcripts outside the skill.

## Methodology status

The sources document the instructor's High Performance methodology and implementation guidance. They do not independently prove scientific validity, health efficacy, professional consensus, or a universal standard of coach competence. Claims made inside the source should not be upgraded into verified facts merely because they appear in a script.

The source materials state that delivery is restricted to appropriately trained or certified practitioners. This skill supports the user's private reflective supervision; it does not grant training rights, certification, or permission to redistribute course material.

## Health and safety

Some energy and physiology materials include exercise, breathwork, diet, hydration, supplementation, sleep, meditation, and medical-testing suggestions. The review may assess whether these appeared with consent, tailoring, and appropriate boundaries. It must not endorse them as individualized medical advice. Flag symptoms, contraindications, eating-disorder concerns, severe distress, or other clinical issues for qualified review.

## Session privacy

- Prefer pseudonyms or stable case IDs.
- Remove unnecessary identifying details.
- Do not add client material to `references/`.
- Do not retain a client transcript after the requested review unless explicitly asked.
- Do not transmit confidential material externally without explicit authorization.
