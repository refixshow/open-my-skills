# Combined session report

Do not use a numeric score.

## 1. Context and reliability

Name the analyzed artifact, declared or inferred topic, transcript completeness, speaker/timestamp reliability, missing context, and parallel or sequential execution.

## 2. Overall verdict

In 3–6 sentences state whether the session served its intended function, the strongest element, the highest-priority correction, and whether any second-sieve tool added real value.

## 3. What worked

Give 2–4 transcript-grounded strengths. Credit effective adaptation and restraint; do not score script memorization.

## 4. First sieve — High Performance

Give 2–5 findings ordered by impact. For each include:

1. priority (`P1 - High Performance` or `No action`);
2. timestamp or locator;
3. direct observation;
4. functional interpretation;
5. likely client/session cost or benefit;
6. one supervision hypothesis or better move;
7. source basis and confidence.

End with a topic-discipline conclusion that distinguishes useful bridges from scope drift.

## 5. Second sieve — coaching tools

Give zero to three admitted opportunities. For each include moment, client signal, tool ID/name, connection to the current objective, one concise intervention, simpler alternative, source basis, and confidence. Label admitted items `P2 - Coaching craft`.

If none pass, say that no additional tool was needed. Do not manufacture a gap.

## 6. Client Observation Sheet draft

Complete all fields from [forms.md](forms.md). Use concise paraphrases and timestamp anchors; do not copy sensitive narrative.

## 7. Coach Self-Evaluation Sheet

Render all eight first-person strength and improvement prompts from [forms.md](forms.md), each followed by `Coach response: ____`. Then render the Clarity, Energy, Courage, Productivity, and Influence first-person prompts, each followed by `Coach response: ____` and `Rating: __/10`.

Do not answer these prompts from the transcript, provide evidence or counterevidence inside this section, or infer how the coach privately felt. Keep transcript-grounded supervision in sections 3–5 and longitudinal evidence in section 8.

## 8. Longitudinal progress

Include only in Development mode. For each active focus with a comparable opportunity give target behavior, progress evidence, counterevidence, verdict, and next observable behavior. Use `insufficient_evidence` when appropriate.

## 9. Practice priorities

Give at most three behavioral priorities in priority-gate order. Name the real-session trigger and portable rule. Do not reveal all stored prompts.

If practice starts, end with exactly:

```text
Ćwiczenie 1
Pytanie 1: <one prompt>
```

Then wait.
