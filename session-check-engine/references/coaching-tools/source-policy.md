# Source, privacy, and scope policy

## Two-source rule

Each topic has two independent sources:

- `TNN-PDF`: the PDF paired with episode NN;
- `TNN-AUDIO`: the lecture audio paired with episode NN.

Record both in the topic evidence map. Use page numbers for PDF claims and timestamps for audio claims. When only one source supports a detail, cite only that source. When they differ, preserve the difference and lower confidence as needed.

Use [source-manifest.md](source-manifest.md) to resolve source IDs to original filenames.

## Distillation rule

Store only concise, original procedural knowledge in the skill:

- indications and non-indications;
- observable session signals;
- process steps;
- example interventions written afresh;
- cautions and boundaries;
- a provenance map.

Keep original PDFs, MP3 files, extracted text, and full transcripts outside the skill. Do not reproduce substantial source passages.

## Methodology status

The paired lectures and PDFs document the course author's methodology. They do not by themselves establish scientific validity, clinical efficacy, professional consensus, or legal scope of practice. Attribute a technique to the course when relevant and keep the skill's safety boundaries even when a source demonstrates a broader or more directive use.

## Session-data rule

Treat client transcripts as confidential working inputs:

- prefer pseudonyms or stable case IDs;
- remove contact, employer, health, and other identifying details when not needed;
- do not add client material to `references/`;
- do not retain a transcript after the requested review unless the user explicitly asks;
- do not transmit it to an external service without explicit authorization.

## Scope rule

This skill supports reflective coaching supervision. It does not establish coach competence, client diagnosis, therapeutic formulation, or crisis risk. When the transcript contains safety, abuse, self-harm, severe impairment, or other material outside ordinary coaching scope, stop tool matching for that passage and recommend qualified human review.
